## Product photos

Everything in `public/images/products/` is the client's own product photography, downloaded at full size from the original Naadu Nalam store catalogue.

Full-size originals are kept outside the repo in `../source-images/`. Files in `public/` are resized WebP copies built by `node scripts/optimize-images.mjs`; edit or add originals there and re-run the script rather than committing large files to `public/`.
